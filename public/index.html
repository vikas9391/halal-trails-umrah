FAQ & TERMS HIGHLIGHTS

Before you reserve.

What dates should my passport be valid through?

The registration logic requires passport validity of at least six months after the December 2, 2026 trip end, meaning an expiration date of June 2, 2027 or later.

What is the cancellation policy?

A non-refundable deposit holds your place. Cancellations before September 24, 2026 receive a 50% refund of amounts paid beyond the deposit; after that date, the submitted terms state no refunds. Once an airline ticket is issued in your name, you are responsible for the full package cost.

How is rooming handled?

Quad is the base rate. Rooming is assigned by the travel team, with family units and roommate requests considered. Triple, double and single upgrades are subject to inventory.

Are the hotel names guaranteed?

No. The public package promises premium 5-star lodging in Madinah and Makkah. Final hotels may change based on group availability, with comparable quality targeted.

How are card payments handled?

Card payments use Stripe Checkout. Payment data is entered directly into Stripe’s secure payment experience rather than the Halal Trails server.
